---
id: adjective-before-vowel
name: Adjectives that change shape before a vowel
category: adjectives
---

Three common adjectives have a third form used only in front of a masculine
noun starting with a vowel or a silent «h». It exists purely so the words don't
collide when you say them.

- vieux → **vieil** : un vieil homme, un vieil ami. But un vieux monsieur.
- beau → **bel** : un bel été. But un beau jardin.
- nouveau → **nouvel** : un nouvel appartement. But un nouveau bureau.

The feminine forms already end in a consonant sound — vieille, belle, nouvelle —
so they never need this and never change.
