---
id: reported-speech-que
name: «que» is never optional
category: verbs
---

English lets you drop "that" when reporting speech or thought. French does not:
the «que» has to be written, and it shortens to «qu'» before a vowel.

- Il dit **qu'**il avait raté le train. — He said (that) he'd missed it.
- Je crois **que** tu as raison. — I think you're right.
- Elle sait **qu'**il ment. — She knows he's lying.

Leaving it out is one of the clearest signs of an English sentence wearing
French words.
