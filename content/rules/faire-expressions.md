id: faire-expressions
name: «faire» does the work of many English verbs
category: verbs
---

French leans on «faire» — to do, to make — where English has a specific verb.
These are set phrases; the pieces don't translate one by one.

- **faire non de la tête** — to shake one's head. Literally: to make no with the head.
- **faire la queue** — to queue.
- **faire attention** — to pay attention.
- Il **fait** froid. — It's cold.

Learn each as a block. Assembling them from the English is how you end up with
sentences no one says.
