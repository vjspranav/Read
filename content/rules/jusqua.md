---
id: jusqua
name: «jusqu'à» — all the way to
category: prepositions
---

«à» tells you the destination. «jusqu'à» tells you the whole distance or
duration was covered — up to and including that point.

- Il marcha **jusqu'à** la gare. — He walked the entire way there.
- Elle a attendu **jusqu'à** midi. — Right up until noon.
- **jusqu'au** bout — all the way to the end. («à + le» contracts as usual.)

It behaves like «à» afterwards: «jusqu'au», «jusqu'aux», and «jusqu'en» before
a year or a month.
