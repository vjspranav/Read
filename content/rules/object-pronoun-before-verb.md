---
id: object-pronoun-before-verb
name: Object pronouns go in front of the verb
category: pronouns
---

Replace a noun with a pronoun and it moves to the left, in front of the verb
group. English keeps it after the verb; French almost never does.

- Elle ne **lui** avait jamais posé de questions. — asked **him**.
- Je **le** vois. — I see **him**.
- Il **nous** a écrit. — He wrote to **us**.

In a compound tense it lands before the helper, not before the participle:
«il **l'**a vu», never «il a **l'**vu». The one place it goes after the verb is
a positive command: «regarde-**moi**».
