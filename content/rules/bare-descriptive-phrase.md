---
id: bare-descriptive-phrase
name: A bare noun phrase tacked on to describe a state
category: adjectives
---

French can end a sentence with a comma and a naked noun phrase — no verb, no
"with" — describing the state of the person or thing just mentioned.

- Il rentra, **les chaussures trempées**. — his shoes soaked.
- Le train était là, **portes ouvertes**. — doors open.
- Il s'assit, **son billet à la main**. — ticket in hand.

English needs "with" or a participle to do this. French needs nothing, which is
why it reads as compact and slightly literary.
