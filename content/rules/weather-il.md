---
id: weather-il
name: The empty «il» — weather, time, and there-is
category: pronouns
---

Some French sentences need a subject even when nothing is doing anything. «il»
fills the slot and means nothing, just as "it" does in "it's raining".

- **Il** pleuvait encore. — It was still raining.
- **Il** est sept heures. — It's seven o'clock.
- **Il** faut partir. — We have to go.

This «il» never refers to a person, so it is never «elle», whatever the weather
word is.
