id: body-parts-definite-article
name: «le» where English says "my", "his", "her"
category: articles
---

With parts of the body, and with clothes you're wearing, French uses the plain
definite article. Whose they are is obvious, so saying it would sound heavy.

- les mains dans **les** poches — his hands in his pockets.
- Elle leva **les** yeux. — She looked up (lifted her eyes).
- Il a mal à **la** tête. — His head hurts.

Use «son», «sa», «ses» here and a French reader hears a learner. Save the
possessives for things that could belong to somebody else.
