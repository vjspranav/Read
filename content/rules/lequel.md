---
id: lequel
name: «lequel» — which one
category: pronouns
---

«quel» sits in front of a noun; «lequel» replaces the noun entirely and means
which one. It agrees with whatever it stands for.

- **Quel** train ? — Which train? → **Lequel** ? — Which one?
- masculine: lequel / lesquels — feminine: laquelle / lesquelles.
- Elle ne lui demanda pas **lequel**. — She didn't ask which one.

After «à» and «de» it contracts: «auquel», «duquel», «desquelles».
