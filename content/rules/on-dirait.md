---
id: on-dirait
name: «on dirait» — it looks like, it seems
category: verbs
---

«on dirait» is a fixed phrase French reaches for whenever something looks or
seems like something else. Word for word it's "one would say", but nobody
means that literally — it's just how French says "it looks like" or "it
seems like".

- Avec ce brouillard, **on dirait** la nuit. — With this fog, it looks like
  night.
- **On dirait** qu'il va pleuvoir. — It looks like it's going to rain.
- Ce fromage, **on dirait** du savon. — This cheese looks like soap.

Treat the whole phrase as one block. Don't try to translate «dirait» on its
own as "would say" — together the two words just mean "looks like".
