---
id: avoir-expressions
name: «avoir» does the work of "to be" for how you feel
category: verbs
---

Where English uses "to be" for hunger, thirst, or age, French uses «avoir» —
literally "to have" — plus a bare noun. There's no article: not "avoir de la
faim", just «avoir faim».

- **avoir faim** — to be hungry. Literally: to have hunger.
- **avoir soif** — to be thirsty.
- **avoir froid** / **avoir chaud** — to be cold / to be hot.
- **avoir vingt ans** — to be twenty. Age works the same way.

Learn each one as a fixed pair. Translating "to be hungry" word for word gives
«être faim», which nobody says.
