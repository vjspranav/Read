---
id: duration-pendant-depuis
name: «pendant» and «depuis» — two kinds of "for"
category: prepositions
---

English uses "for" for both. French splits them by whether the thing is over.

- **Pendant** quarante ans, il avait pris ce train. — Finished stretch.
- Il travaille ici **depuis** quarante ans. — Started then, still true.
- Elle en mettait deux **depuis** mardi. — Since Tuesday, still doing it.

The tense follows the choice. «depuis» goes with the present for something
still going on, or the imparfait inside a story — never the tense English uses
("has been"). «pendant» goes with a finished past.
