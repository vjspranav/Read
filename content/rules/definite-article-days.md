---
id: definite-article-days
name: «le mardi» means every Tuesday
category: articles
---

A bare day name points at one particular day. Add «le» and it becomes a
routine — the same thing English does with a plural.

- **mardi**, il part à sept heures. — This coming Tuesday.
- **le** mardi, il part à sept heures. — On Tuesdays, as a rule.
- **le** matin, elle lit le journal. — In the mornings.

Same trick with «le soir», «le week-end», «l'été». Without the article you're
naming an occasion; with it, a habit.
