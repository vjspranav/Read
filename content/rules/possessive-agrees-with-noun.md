---
id: possessive-agrees-with-noun
name: «son» and «sa» agree with the thing, not the owner
category: agreement
---

French possessives take the gender of what is owned. They tell you nothing
about who owns it — «son» is his, her, or its, and only the context decides.

- **son** billet — his ticket or her ticket; «billet» is masculine.
- **sa** femme — his wife; «femme» is feminine.
- **ses** mains — his or her hands; plural.

Before a feminine word starting with a vowel, «sa» becomes «son» for the sound:
«**son** amie» is still a female friend.
