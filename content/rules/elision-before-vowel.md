---
id: elision-before-vowel
name: Small words drop their vowel before another vowel
category: articles
---

A handful of one-syllable words lose their final vowel when the next word
begins with a vowel or a silent «h». The apostrophe is what's left of it. This
is not optional and not casual — writing the full form would be a mistake.

- le, la → **l'** : l'homme, l'avait entendu.
- de → **d'** : dix minutes d'avance.
- ne → **n'** : il n'y avait plus rien.
- que → **qu'** : il dit qu'il avait raté le train.

«l'» hides which word it came from, so «l'» can be the, him, or her, and only
the sentence tells you which.
