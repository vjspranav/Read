---
id: reflexive-passive
name: «se» when nobody in particular is doing it
category: verbs
---

French often uses «se» to say something happens, without naming who caused it.
English uses the plain verb, or a passive.

- Les portes **se** fermèrent. — The doors closed.
- Ça **se** dit. — People say that.
- Le vin blanc **se** boit frais. — White wine is drunk chilled.

Without the «se», «les portes fermèrent» would claim the doors closed something
else. The «se» is what turns the action back on the subject.
