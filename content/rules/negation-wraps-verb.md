---
id: negation-wraps-verb
name: Negation wraps the conjugated verb
category: negation
---

French negates with a pair: «ne» before the verb, «pas» after it. In compound
tenses the pair closes around the small verb that carries the tense, not around
the participle that carries the meaning.

- Je **ne** mange **pas**. — I'm not eating.
- Je **n'**ai **pas** mangé. — I haven't eaten. Never «je n'ai mangé pas».
- Elle **ne** l'avait **pas** entendu partir. — «pas» lands after «avait».

Object pronouns stay inside the sandwich too, tucked between «ne» and the verb:
«je **ne** le vois **pas**».
