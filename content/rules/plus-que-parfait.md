---
id: plus-que-parfait
name: One step further back — avait fait
category: verbs
---

When a story in the past needs to mention something that happened even earlier,
French uses «avait» or «était» plus the participle. It works exactly like
English "had done".

- Elle ne l'avait pas entendu partir. — He left; her not-hearing came after.
- Il **avait pris** ce train pendant quarante ans. — Before the story starts.
- Il **s'était arrêté** au coin de la rue. — Before he reached the station.

Which helper you use is the same choice as in the passé composé: «être» for
reflexives and for the come-and-go verbs, «avoir» for everything else.
