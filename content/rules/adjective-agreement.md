---
id: adjective-agreement
name: Adjectives match the noun in gender and number
category: agreement
---

An adjective takes an «e» for a feminine noun and an «s» for a plural, in
whatever order applies. You often can't hear it; you always have to write it.

- portes **ouvertes** — feminine and plural, so «-es».
- les chaussures **trempées** — same, and the participle behaves like an adjective.
- un banc **vert**, une porte **verte**, des portes **vertes**.

Adjectives already ending in «e» add only the «s»: «une rue calme», «des rues
calmes».
