---
id: pronoun-en
name: «en» stands in for «de» plus a thing
category: pronouns
---

«en» replaces a noun introduced by «de», «du», «des», or by a number or
quantity. English usually just leaves the noun out; French insists on marking
the gap.

- Elle **en** mettait deux. — She put out two (of them).
- Tu veux du café ? — J'**en** veux bien. — I'd like some.
- Il **en** revient. — He's coming back from there.

Like other object pronouns it sits in front of the verb, and it stays even when
a number follows: «j'**en** ai trois», never «j'ai trois».
