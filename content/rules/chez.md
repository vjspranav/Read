---
id: chez
name: «chez» — at someone's place
category: prepositions
---

«chez» has no English equivalent. It means at or to the home, shop, or world
of the person named after it, and it must be followed by a person, not a place.

- **chez** lui — at his place, home.
- Je vais **chez** le médecin. — To the doctor's.
- **chez** Renault — at Renault, as an employer.

You cannot say «chez la maison». For a building you use «à»: «à la maison».
