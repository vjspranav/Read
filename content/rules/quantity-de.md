---
id: quantity-de
name: «beaucoup de», «un peu de» — quantity words skip the partitive
category: articles
---

Once a word already says how much — a lot, a little, too much, a kilo —
French doesn't also reach for «du», «de la», or «des». It drops straight to
bare «de» (or «d'» before a vowel). Saying both would be naming the amount
twice.

- **un peu d'**argent — a little money. Never «un peu de l'argent».
- **beaucoup de** monde — a lot of people.
- **un kilo de** pommes — a kilo of apples.

Compare «elle achète du pain» — some bread, no amount given, so the partitive
stays — with «elle achète beaucoup de pain» — a lot of bread, the amount is
already named by «beaucoup», so «du» disappears.
