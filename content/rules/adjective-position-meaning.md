---
id: adjective-position-meaning
name: Adjectives that change meaning when they move
category: adjectives
---

Most French adjectives follow the noun. A few can go either side, and the side
changes what they mean — before the noun the sense is figurative, after it the
sense is literal.

- son **ancien** bureau (his former office) / un bureau **ancien** (an antique desk)
- un **grand** homme (a great man) / un homme **grand** (a tall man)
- mon **propre** livre (my own book) / un livre **propre** (a clean book)

The same list includes cher, pauvre, and dernier. When in doubt, put the
adjective after — that's where the plain meaning lives.
