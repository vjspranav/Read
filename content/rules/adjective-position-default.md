name: Most adjectives follow the noun; a handful of short, common ones go before
category: adjectives
---

In French the noun usually comes first and the describing word follows it —
that's true for colour, shape, and most other adjectives. A short list of
very common adjectives about beauty, age, goodness, and size breaks that
rule and sits in front of the noun instead: beau, joli, vieux, jeune, bon,
mauvais, grand, petit, gros, nouveau.

When one adjective from each group describes the same noun, the short one
goes first and the colour or shape word stays after, with the noun
sandwiched between them.

- un **vieux** vélo **jaune** — old goes before the noun, yellow stays after it.
- une **jolie** maison **rouge** — pretty goes before, red stays after.
- un **grand** chien **noir** — big goes before, black stays after.
