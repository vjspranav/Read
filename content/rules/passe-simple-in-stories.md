id: passe-simple-in-stories
name: The written past — ferma, marcha, partit
category: verbs
---

French has a past tense that is only ever written: novels, history books,
fairy tales. Nobody speaks it. Where a conversation says «il a fermé», a story
says «il ferma» — same meaning, different register.

- Il **ferma** la porte. — In speech: il a fermé la porte.
- Il **marcha** jusqu'à la gare. — In speech: il a marché.
- Les portes **se fermèrent**. — In speech: elles se sont fermées.

You need to recognise it, not produce it. Most verbs are predictable: -er verbs
end in -a (il parla), and a lot of others end in -it (il partit, il dit).
