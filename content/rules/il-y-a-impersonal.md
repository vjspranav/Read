---
id: il-y-a-impersonal
name: «il y a» — there is, there are
category: verbs
---

Three words that only mean anything together. Word for word it's "it there
has", which is no help; take it as one block meaning there is or there are.

- **Il y a** deux tasses sur la table. — There are two cups.
- **Il y avait** plus personne. — There was no one left.
- **Il y a** trois ans — three years ago. Same phrase, time meaning.

It never becomes plural — «il y a deux tasses», never «ils y ont». Only the
tense of «avoir» changes: il y avait, il y aura, il y a eu.
