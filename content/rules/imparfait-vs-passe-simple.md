---
id: imparfait-vs-passe-simple
name: Imparfait or passé simple — the choice that carries the story
category: verbs
---

Both are past. The imparfait (-ait, -aient) is unfinished: the setting, the
habit, the state, what was going on. The passé simple — or the passé composé in
speech — is one completed event that moves the story forward.

- Il **pleuvait**. Il **ferma** la porte. — Rain all around; one closed door.
- Le train **partait** sans lui, comme chaque mardi. — The weekly pattern.
- Le train **partit** sans lui. — One departure, over.

Same verb, different frame. Ask whether you're describing the picture or
adding the next thing that happened.
