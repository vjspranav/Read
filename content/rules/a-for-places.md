---
id: a-for-places
name: Verbs that need «à» before a place
category: prepositions
---

Some French verbs come with a preposition attached, and you can't drop it even
when English has none. «arriver» is one: you always arrive **at**.

- Il arriva **à** la gare. — He reached the station.
- Elle est entrée **dans** la cuisine. — She entered the kitchen.
- Nous allons **à** Rouen. — We're going to Rouen.

Cities take «à», feminine countries take «en» (en France), masculine ones take
«au» (au Portugal).
