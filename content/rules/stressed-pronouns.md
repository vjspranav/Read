---
id: stressed-pronouns
name: The standalone pronouns — moi, toi, lui, elle, eux
category: pronouns
---

French has two sets of pronouns. The little ones (le, la, lui, me) can only
live glued to a verb. After a preposition, or standing alone, you need the
strong forms: moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, eux, elles.

- Le train partait sans **lui**. — Never «sans le».
- Je pense à **elle**. — I'm thinking about her.
- Qui a fait ça ? — **Moi**. — Me.

They're also what you use for emphasis: «**Moi**, je reste» — me, I'm staying.
