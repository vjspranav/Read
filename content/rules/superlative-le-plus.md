---
id: superlative-le-plus
name: «le plus» — building the superlative
category: adjectives
---

French doesn't add an ending like English "-est". It puts «le/la/les plus» in
front of the adjective instead, and the adjective still agrees with its noun
the way it always does.

- le pot **le plus** proche — the closest jar.
- la maison **la plus** grande — the biggest house.
- les rues **les plus** calmes — the quietest streets.

A few common adjectives are irregular and skip «plus» entirely: «bon»
becomes «meilleur» (better) and «le meilleur» (the best) — never «le plus
bon».
