---
id: reflexive-verbs
name: Verbs that act on themselves — se lever, s'arrêter
category: verbs
---

A lot of everyday French verbs carry a «se». It marks that the subject is also
the target: you lift yourself, you stop yourself. English usually has a
separate word for the same idea and no pronoun at all.

- lever (to lift) → **se lever** — to get up.
- arrêter (to stop something) → **s'arrêter** — to come to a halt.
- presser → **se presser** — to hurry.

The pronoun changes with the subject (je **me** lève, tu **te** lèves), and in
compound tenses these verbs always take «être»: «il **s'était** arrêté».
