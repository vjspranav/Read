---
id: preposition-plus-infinitive
name: After a preposition, French uses the infinitive
category: prepositions
---

Where English puts an "-ing" form after a preposition, French puts the plain
dictionary form of the verb.

- **sans** se presser — without hurrying.
- **pour** l'attendre — to wait for him / for waiting for him.
- **avant de** partir — before leaving.

The one exception is «en», which takes the -ant form: «en partant» — while
leaving. Everything else — sans, pour, avant de, après avoir, au lieu de —
takes the infinitive.
