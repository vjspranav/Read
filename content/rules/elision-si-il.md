---
id: elision-si-il
name: «si» shortens only before «il» and «ils»
category: pronouns
---

«si» meaning if keeps its vowel almost everywhere. The single exception is in
front of «il» and «ils».

- Elle demanda **s'il** montait. — if he was getting on.
- Elle demanda **s'ils** montaient. — if they were.
- But: **si elle** montait, **si on** partait, **si un** train arrivait.

Because of this, «s'il» looks identical to the «s'» of «s'arrêter» and is a
completely different word. Check what follows: a pronoun subject means «if».
